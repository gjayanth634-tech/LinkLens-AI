What We Analyze

LinkLens AI leverages advanced Machine Learning and static lexical inspection to detect phishing risks based strictly on URL patterns:

URL Structure

Overall length, path depth, hostname dimensions, subdomains count, and token segmentation.

Character Analysis

Frequency and distributions of dots (.), hyphens (-), digits, percent symbols (%), and delimiters.

Security Indicators

HTTPS protocol validation, IP address hostname identification, and credential indicators (@ symbol).

Lexical Patterns

Presence of deceitful keywords (e.g. login, verify, banking), hyphenated brand names, and spoofed TLDs.

Zero-Risk Guarantee: We never visit, load, or connect to the submitted URL. All heuristics are computed statically in an isolated environment.
